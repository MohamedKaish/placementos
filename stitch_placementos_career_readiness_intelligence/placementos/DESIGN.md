## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an institutional, high-conviction visual language engineered for talent readiness analytics, technical skill benchmarking, and workforce intelligence. It rejects frivolous gamification, cartoonish rewards, and loud consumer gradients in favor of an authoritative, data-dense, and executive-ready aesthetic. The product operates at the intersection of elite software engineering standards and strategic talent evaluation; therefore, the visual register reflects scientific precision, forensic clarity, and corporate resilience.

The design movement combines **Modern High-Density Technical SaaS** with **Architectonic Minimalism**:
- **Foundation**: Dense, high-contrast framing where deep graphite structures anchor crystalline light cards and precise data matrices.
- **Atmosphere**: Serious, analytical, and definitive. The interface signals mathematical validity and institutional trust to enterprise hiring leaders while projecting high-stakes career trajectory to elite candidates.
- **Restraint**: Zero decorative noise. Meaningful visual weight is strictly reserved for actionable readiness scores, empirical gap analyses, and verified credential paths.

## Colors

The palette leverages a calibrated balance of deep foundation anchors, hyper-clean technical surfaces, and rigorous semantic signaling:

- **Brand Primary & Secondary**:
  - `Electric Blue` (`#3B82F6`): The primary driver for key interactive moments, focused states, and navigational prominence.
  - `Indigo Surge` (`#6366F1`): Secondary driver applied to cohort intelligence highlights, diagnostic metrics, and progression indicators.
- **Foundation & Neutrals**:
  - `Foundation Base` (`#090D14`): Deep structural canvas for tooltips, terminal overlays, and top-tier persistent navigational frameworks.
  - `Slate Heavy` (`#0F172A` / `#1E293B`): Deep enterprise ink, primary header typography, and crisp edge definitions.
  - `Slate Mid` (`#334155` / `#64748B` / `#94A3B8`): Muted copy, metadata, tabular headers, inactive strokes, and secondary indicators.
  - `Surface Pristine` (`#FFFFFF` / `#F8FAFC`): Clinical card surfaces and background canvas that provide maximum optical separation.
- **Semantic Evidence Engine**:
  - `Verified / Demonstrated` (`#10B981`): Precision emerald indicating cryptographically or empirically verified skill masteries.
  - `Developing / Claimed` (`#F59E0B`): Technical amber indicating unverified claims, in-progress assessments, or calibration anomalies.
  - `Calibration Gap / Critical` (`#F43F5E`): Surgical crimson signaling verified knowledge shortfalls, failure to hit hiring bars, and systemic rubric disparities.

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes a tri-family architectural framework to establish immediate optical hierarchy between narrative headlines, high-readability evaluation matrices, and empirical system telemetry:

- **Display & Sectioning (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers geometric clarity and structural authority. High-density tracking offsets optical expansion, keeping headlines compact and executive-grade.
- **Narrative & UI Prose (`Inter`)**: Deployed for comprehensive rubrics, student portfolios, interview annotations, and form structures. Highly legible at dense UI scales with neutral humanist qualities.
- **Telemetry & Scores (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Strict tabular alignment for percentages, percentile indexes, skill confidence scores, and code telemetry. Monospaced styling reinforces scientific measurement rather than subjective scoring.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model executes a precision 12-column fluid grid governed by rigid enterprise density tiers:

- **Desktop Form Factor (>1280px)**: 12 columns with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and a minimum 32px (`2rem`) outer margin. Maximum canvas container caps at 1440px to ensure data density without line-length degradation.
- **Tablet & Compact Workspace (768px - 1279px)**: 8 columns with 16px (`1rem`) gutters. Sidebars fold into an off-canvas drawer or high-density rail (`64px` icon bar).
- **Mobile Handheld (<768px)**: 4 columns with 12px (`0.75rem`) gutters and 16px (`1rem`) viewport margins. Multicolumn evaluation grids collapse into sequential modular evidence cards.
- **Rhythm**: Strict 4px and 8px base units preserve mathematical harmony across metric tiles, data tables, and verification matrices. Element spacing favors compact groupings (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) within analytical units, offset by definitive structural pauses (`space-lg` and `space-xl`) between independent assessment categories.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is articulated through **Tonal Layers** and **Low-Contrast Precision Outlines**, completely bypassing heavy, diffuse shadows:

- **Base Platform Canvas**: Rendered in cool neutral slate (`#F8FAFC`), providing an ultra-crisp negative space.
- **Card & Evaluation Containers**: Solid white (`#FFFFFF`) surfaces framed with razor-thin, 1px high-precision borders (`#E2E8F0` or `#CBD5E1`).
- **Surface Elevation Hierarchy**:
  - `Level 0 (Base Canvas)`: `#F8FAFC`, no borders.
  - `Level 1 (Telemetry Cards & Tables)`: `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, no shadow.
  - `Level 2 (Hover Focus & Active Inspection)`: `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#94A3B8`, subtle hairline directional shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
  - `Level 3 (Diagnostic Overlays & Flyout Panels)`: `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#64748B`, structural shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **High-Density Focus Surfaces**: Deep graphite floating modules (`#090D14` and `#0F172A`) reserved for raw code diffs, command-line evidence, and live-interview telemetry indicators.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Soft / Engineering Precision (`1`)** shape language:

- **Components**: Standard components (buttons, input fields, interactive badges, table rows) feature an exact `4px` (`0.25rem`) radius. This eliminates playful softness while maintaining an approachable modern aesthetic.
- **Surface Modules**: Large analytical cards, benchmark charts, and modal viewports use `rounded-lg` at `8px` (`0.5rem`).
- **Telemetry Indicators**: Pure circular dots (`50%` radius) are strictly reserved for live connection statuses and micro status beacons. Pill shapes are banned across the system to maintain angular, data-first discipline.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#3B82F6` or `#0F172A` fill with high-contrast text (`#FFFFFF`), 4px border radius, 0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05) shadow. Subtle hover transition shifts hue to `#2563EB` or `#1E293B`.
- **Secondary / Technical**: 1px border in `#CBD5E1`, transparent fill, text in `#0F172A`. On hover: subtle `#F1F5F9` background fill.
- **Destructive / Diagnostic Gap**: Crimson outline (`#F43F5E`) or subtle crimson-tinted background (`#FFF1F2`) with `#E11D48` text.

### Badges & Semantic Chips
- Flat, low-saturation backgrounds paired with sharp, high-contrast borders:
  - *Verified*: `#ECFDF5` background, `#A7F3D0` border, `#047857` text, prepended with a monospaced check or discrete micro-dot.
  - *Claimed / In-Progress*: `#FFFBEB` background, `#FDE68A` border, `#B45309` text.
  - *Calibration Gap*: `#FFF1F2` background, `#FECDD3` border, `#BE123C` text.
- Badges use `label-sm` (`JetBrains Mono`) for immediate diagnostic authenticity.

### Cards & Data Modules
- Structured white cards with hairline borders (`#E2E8F0`). 
- Card headers feature clean separation: title in `headline-sm`, contextual monospaced metric right-aligned, followed by a 1px divider spanning the full module width.
- Dense inner padding: 16px to 24px (`space-md` to `space-lg`).

### Data Tables & Readiness Matrices
- Compact rows (36px to 44px height) with light horizontal rules (`#F1F5F9`).
- Column headers rendered in `label-sm` with upper tracking, uppercase styling, and subtle directional sorting chevrons.
- Monospaced alignment for numerical scores, percentiles, and technical durations to ensure scannable vertical comparisons.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid border `#CBD5E1`, 4px radius.
- Focus state: Crisp 1px border `#3B82F6` with a uniform 2px outline ring in `#DBEAFE` (`rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.25)`).
- Typography strictly leverages `body-md` for text entry and `label-sm` for system labels.

### Readiness Gauge & Metric Micro-Tiles
- Specialized readouts displaying percentile thresholds without circular ring charts.
- Linear multi-segmented progress bars using semantic color segments (`#10B981`, `#F59E0B`, `#F43F5E`) demarcated by 1px white vertical notches.
- Tabular metric output displays score absolute values alongside delta thresholds (`+4.2% vs Bar`).
